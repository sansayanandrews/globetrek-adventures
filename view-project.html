A dynamic web-based travel management platform built for GlobeTrek Adventures in Negombo, Sri Lanka. Compliance with 8 Stated Assumptions

1. Currency in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) Strictly quoted and calculated in LKR across public tour cards, dynamic customizations, booking vouchers, and executive revenue charts.
2. Simulated Payment Gateway Validates non-sensitive dummy card parameters and produces unique transaction references formatted as GT-TXN-XXXXXX without external card processor dependencies.
3. Internal Coordination Model Maintains partner hotels and private transport in local database tables. Staff update bespoke driver and hotel booking coordination notes per itinerary.
4. Simulated Notifications In-app Notification records track booking updates and query responses, viewable directly from the customer dashboard without external SMS/SMTP servers.
5. Working Mockups & Screenshots The functional React web application serves as the mockup deliverable, with all 25 screens systematically captured via Puppeteer into docs/screenshots/.
6. Seed Accounts & Real Packages Pre-seeded with 3 roles (Customer, Staff, Admin) and authentic Sri Lankan tour routes (Sigiriya Citadel, Ella Highlands, Yala Safari, Galle Fort, etc.).
7. Single Agency Scope Dedicated exclusively to GlobeTrek Adventures in Negombo in English, preventing scope bloat while delivering depth across all operational workflows.
8. Usability Evaluation Template Supplied with a blank 8-item usability questionnaire and structured evaluation write-up template with zero fabricated test responses.
